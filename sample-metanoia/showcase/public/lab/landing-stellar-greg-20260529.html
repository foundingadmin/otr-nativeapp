The questions worth answering before you decide

We know moving forward takes more than a good demo. Here are the concerns that come up most — answered directly.

How long does implementation actually take?

Most customers are live within 30 days. Our catalog conversion team handles digitizing your existing PDFs and OEM files — your team's time investment is typically under 8 hours total during onboarding.

Will it work with our existing systems?

AIC integrates with every major ERP and CMMS — SAP, Oracle, Infor, Maximo, and more. Most integrations are completed in under 48 hours. We maintain and support them at no additional cost.

How do we get our team to actually adopt it?

Adoption is our problem to solve, not yours. Every account includes a 2-hour live onboarding session, a dedicated customer success contact for the first 90 days, and in-app guided tours. Average time-to-value is under one week.

What does data security look like?

SOC 2 Type II certified. Your catalog data is stored in isolated, encrypted tenants. We do not share or resell data. SSO/SAML supported. Full audit logs available for your security team.

How do we justify the cost internally?

The average customer recovers the annual cost within 90 days, driven by reduced unplanned downtime and time saved per technician per shift. We'll model the ROI specific to Stellar's team size and asset count before you commit.

What happens when our parts data changes?

AIC stays current automatically. OEM supersessions are monitored and surfaced in real time. Your internal inventory syncs from your ERP on a schedule you define — no manual updates required.
