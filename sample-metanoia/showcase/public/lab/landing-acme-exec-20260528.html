The problem

Parts searches shouldn't take half a shift

Operations leaders at industrial enterprises lose real money every week to the same three problems.

Outdated paper catalogs

Printed part books go stale the moment a supersession is issued. Technicians order discontinued parts and don't find out until the box arrives.

Parts scattered across sites

Knowing which warehouse holds a spare means calling each site individually — if anyone picks up. That's hours of downtime waiting on a phone call.

Unplanned downtime costs

Every hour of unplanned downtime is real money. The bottleneck usually isn't the repair — it's finding the right part number first.
